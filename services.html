Rider Longevity & Protocol

Maintenance & Battery Safety Master Guide

High-voltage lithium batteries and high-torque motors require disciplined maintenance to stay safe and preserve battery health for 5+ years.

1. Charging Fire Safety

  • Never charge unattended: Do not leave plugged in overnight while sleeping.
  • OEM chargers only: Cheap aftermarket bricks lack proper thermal cutoff circuits.
  • Inspect casing: If your battery is dropped or shows physical dents, retire immediately.

2. Hydraulic Brake Bleeds

  • Check pad thickness: Replace brake pads before they wear below 1.0mm thickness.
  • Mineral oil bleeds: Bleed hydraulic brake lines every 12 months to prevent spongy levers.
  • Inspect rotor truing: Ensure 180mm/203mm rotors do not rub against callipers.

3. Mid-Drive Drivetrain Care

  • Ease pedal pressure: Pause pedaling slightly during gear shifts to protect cassette teeth.
  • Use e-bike chains: Reinforced pin chains (e.g. KMC e10/e11) withstand 90Nm motor torque.
  • Lubricate weekly: Dry-lube for summer dust, wet-lube for rainy winter commuting.
